Before You Prequalify

ClearPath Financial's mortgage prequalification tool is currently available to residents of most U.S. states; it is not available in New York or Hawaii at this time. Prequalifying requires a minimum credit score of 620 and verifiable income documentation.

Prequalification gives you an early estimate of what you may be able to borrow. It is not a loan offer, and it does not obligate any lender to extend credit to you.
